About Softora

A product company that also builds to order.

We started by building software for one school. Then the school next door asked. Academy came out of that. The custom work came from everyone else who asked.

Our story

Software should fit the place it's used.

Most of the software sold to institutions and businesses here was designed somewhere else, for someone else. It shows: the wrong calendar, the wrong payment methods, menus nobody in the office can read.

We build the other kind. Academy exists because principals, accountants and teachers told us what they actually needed, and kept telling us. Our custom work follows the same rule: sit with the people who do the job, then build for them.

We're a team of engineers, designers and former school administrators based in Dhaka, working with clients across Bangladesh and increasingly beyond it.

How we work

Four things we don't compromise on.

01

Fixed scope, fixed price

You know what you're getting and what it costs before we start. Changes are agreed in writing, not discovered in the invoice.

02

Working software weekly

From week three, there's always something to click. Problems surface early, when they're cheap to fix.

03

Your data is yours

Full export any time. If you leave, you leave with everything. We'd rather earn the renewal.

04

A named person

Support means a person who knows your system, not a ticket number. That's true for Academy and for every custom build.

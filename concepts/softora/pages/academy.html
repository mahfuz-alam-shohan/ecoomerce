Common questions

Before you ask.

How is Academy priced?

Per student, per year, with the modules you switch on. There's a one-time setup fee that covers migration and training. You'll get a written quote after the demo.

How long does it take to go live?

Most institutions are fully live within six weeks of signing. Attendance and fees are usually running in the first two.

Can you move our existing data?

Yes. Spreadsheets, an older system, or paper registers. We clean it, import it, and check it with your office before go-live.

Does it work for colleges and training centres?

Yes. Semesters, credit-based courses, batch intakes and certificate programmes are all supported alongside the school calendar.

What if we need something Academy doesn't do?

That's where the other half of Softora comes in. We build custom modules and integrations on top of Academy for institutions with specific needs.

Questions

Good to know.

How is Academy priced?

Pricing depends on the number of students and the modules you turn on. You'll get a written quote after a short demo, with no hidden charges.

How long does setup take?

Most institutions start using attendance and fees within the first couple of weeks. We plan the full rollout with you at the start.

Can you move our existing data?

Yes. From spreadsheets, an older system or paper registers. We import it and check it with your office before you go live.

Does it work for colleges and coaching centres?

Yes. Semesters, batches, courses and certificate programmes are supported alongside the regular school year.

What if we need a feature that isn't there?

Tell us. Softora also builds custom software, so we can add modules or integrations made for your institution.

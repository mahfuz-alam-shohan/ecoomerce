Our story

Rooted at the crossroads.

Lantz Corners sits where two old highways cross in the middle of the Allegheny forest. People have always needed a good meal here.

01
The crossroads

Route 6 meets Route 219.

Truckers, hunters, families on a fall drive, crews heading to work. Everyone passes through Lantz Corners, and plenty of them stop at the Barrel House.

Kane, the Black Cherry Capital of the World, is just down the road.

02
The room

Built on oil-boom history.

Rustic wood, warm light, and walls that tell the story of the region's oil-boom days. It feels like a place that's been here a long time, because it has.

03
The people

Family-run, next door to family.

The Barrel House sits right beside Lantz Corners Getaway, the family-owned motel and conference center. Stay the night, walk over for breakfast.

Friendly, quick when it's busy, and generous with the coffee. That's what guests keep writing about.

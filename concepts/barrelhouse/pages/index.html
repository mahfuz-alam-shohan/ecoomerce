The corner place

Big portions. Fair prices. No hurry.

Locals come for breakfast and stay for the coffee. Travelers stop on the way back from the Kinzua Bridge and leave with pie. The bar side has the game on. The dining room is quiet enough for the whole family.

The walls carry the oil-boom history of the region, the kitchen keeps things simple, and the folks next door at Lantz Corners Getaway will tell you it's the best meal within a few miles.
